Sime is a fully offline Chinese Pinyin input method.

It supports full Pinyin and T9 layouts, local phrase learning, emoji, clipboard
history, quick phrases, and a local GRU sentence reranker. Input and candidate
ranking remain on the device; the app has no network permission, tracking, or
advertising SDK.

The F-Droid build does not include the optional handwriting recognizer because
its training-data redistribution terms are not suitable for F-Droid.
